Research

Deformable Object Manipulation

Cables and other deformable objects can take many shapes, and the same action can have different effects depending on how they are grasped. I study how robots can choose grasps and reshape these objects using visual observations and feedback from interaction.

Physical Context World Models for Robotic Manipulation

I study how a robot can infer hidden properties, such as stiffness, from what happens when it touches or moves an object. I aim to learn representations that help a robot predict the consequences of its actions and choose what to do next.

Force-aware Vision-Language-Action Models

I explore how force and tactile feedback can complement vision and language in robot policies. These signals can help a robot recognize contact and adjust its motion during manipulation.
