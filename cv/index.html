Research

My research spans embodied artificial intelligence, robot manipulation, deformable object manipulation, vision-language-action models, robot learning and world models, active perception, and force-aware learning.

I have worked on deformable linear object manipulation, interaction-conditioned robot policies, tactile sensing, active perception, and vision-language-action learning.
